Technical Design

We design the full water train — from raw water pump and storage through filtration, RO and treated storage to the pumping system — on Pentair Everpure and ClairHydra platforms.

Read the detail
What happens

We design the full treatment train for your water — raw water pump and storage, filtration for sediment and hardness (softening where your water needs it), RO for TDS, treated water storage and a pumping system that delivers it under pressure to point of use, with UV disinfection added where microbial control is needed — built on authorized Pentair Everpure and ClairHydra platforms, with Pentair Everpure bringing 90+ years behind the components.

What we need from you

Sign-off on the proposed design, along with any product or budget preferences you want us to work within.

What you get

An engineering-first proposal that connects the problem, the selected system, the execution scope and the outcome.

Audit-Ready Documentation

Documentation your auditor will actually accept

An AMC isn’t only about keeping the system running — it’s also a paper trail. Every AMC customer gets a quarterly water quality report they can hand an auditor without a scramble.

A quarterly lab report, on file

Every AMC customer receives a quarterly water quality report from a NABL-accredited laboratory, covering the parameters relevant to their site, with before/after treatment values where applicable.

Ready before the auditor asks

FSSAI’s food-service hygiene requirements expect potable water meeting IS 10500 wherever water is used as an ingredient or touches food-contact surfaces, and expect businesses to retain testing records. Current lab reports mean an audit request gets answered on the spot, not a scramble to arrange testing.

An operational baseline too

The same quarterly numbers double as an early warning system — you can see source water drifting, pre-monsoon to post-monsoon or tanker to borewell, before it turns into an equipment problem.

DhwaNeer’s reporting supports your compliance and audit-readiness process — meeting FSSAI requirements remains the food business operator’s responsibility. Applicable testing frequency and scope should be confirmed against your site’s current FSSAI licence conditions and supply arrangement.

Questions, Answered

How-we-work FAQ

The things prospects ask before handing us a project.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A single-outlet filtration or softening system can be delivered in a few days once the water report and design are signed off, while a larger multi-system plant or a multi-site rollout runs over several weeks. You get an indicative schedule with the proposal.

Do you handle plumbing and electrical work too?

Yes. Execution is turnkey — our own team delivers, installs, plumbs and pressure-tests the system and wires it up, then sanitises it before commissioning. You deal with one accountable team, not a chain of separate contractors.

Which brands and components do you use?

Systems are built on authorized Pentair Everpure and ClairHydra platforms, integrated with genuine components such as Astral pipes, Sintex tanks and John Guest fittings. Everything is chosen to be reliable and serviceable.

Do you test our water before designing the system?

Always. We start by lab-testing your water — TDS, hardness, pH, iron and microbes — at an independent laboratory, so the design is engineered for your actual water rather than a catalogue assumption.

Do you provide AMC after handover?

Yes. Support runs on a planned AMC cycle — handover, then service at M3, M6 and M9, then renewal — with consumables and spares planned ahead. It is system-driven, so service continues on schedule even if an engineer moves on.

Do you work outside Mumbai?

Yes. DhwaNeer is based in Mumbai and executes turnkey projects pan-India, including multi-site and multi-city water programmes. Share your city and we will plan the site visit accordingly.

Do you provide water quality reports we can show an FSSAI auditor?

Yes. Every AMC customer receives a quarterly water quality report from a NABL-accredited laboratory, covering the parameters relevant to their site, with before/after treatment values where applicable. It supports your compliance and audit-readiness process — meeting FSSAI requirements remains the food business operator’s responsibility, and the applicable testing frequency and scope should be confirmed against your site’s current FSSAI licence conditions.

How often are the water quality reports issued?

Quarterly, for customers on an AMC. Beyond audit documentation, the same reports give you an operational baseline — a way to see source water drifting, for instance pre- to post-monsoon or tanker versus borewell, before it turns into an equipment problem. Confirm the exact scope and frequency against your site’s current FSSAI licence conditions and supply arrangement, since requirements can vary by site.
